Micron Technology's quarterly results significantly exceeded expectations, sending the stock up nearly 11%. Strong demand for DRAM, NAND, and HBM memory, which are essential for artificial intelligence infrastructure, continues to drive exceptional growth.

Analysts at UBS, Bank of America, Wedbush, and AlphaValue remain optimistic. Micron also expects another acceleration in its business during the current quarter.
