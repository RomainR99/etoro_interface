After several weeks of significant share price volatility, 2CRSi has announced the launch of an independent verification procedure regarding the sale of 194 servers to a German customer, a contract that was originally announced on June 9, 2026.

The review, approved by the Board of Directors following a recommendation from the Audit and Risk Committee, aims to confirm both the existence and the proper execution of the transaction. To achieve this, direct confirmations will be obtained from independent third parties, including:
- banking institutions,
- the customer,
- logistics and transportation providers.

The objective is to independently verify the purchase order, deliveries, and financial settlement through cross-confirmation. The company emphasizes that this is a factual findings engagement , separate from the statutory audit performed by its external auditors.

The market appears to be responding positively to this initiative. In just five trading sessions, 2CRSi's share price has surged :
nearly 48%, reaching €37.70.

Investors are now awaiting the outcome of this independent verification, which could help provide greater clarity on the transaction. Meanwhile, the company stated that it continues to address the other issues previously raised and will keep the market informed of further developments.
