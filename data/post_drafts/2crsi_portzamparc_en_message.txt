While 2CRSi shares remain suspended following the publication of a highly critical report by Grizzly Research, analysts at Portzamparc believe investors should take a step back before drawing definitive conclusions.

According to Portzamparc, the short seller's report is "well-structured," but some of its conclusions appear excessive. The analysts acknowledge legitimate questions regarding Atlas Cloud's financial strength and its ability to fulfill the announced partnership with New York Green Cloud (NYGC). However, they consider the claim that 2CRSi "fabricated the vast majority of its revenue" to be highly questionable.

Portzamparc highlights several points:

• NYGC's historical contribution to 2CRSi's revenue has been limited.
• The company's industrial activity has been verified over the years through meetings with suppliers, partners, and customers.
• Long-standing relationships with major chip manufacturers, particularly Nvidia, are easily verifiable.

Another interesting point: among the last 20 companies targeted by Grizzly Research, 10 stocks currently trade above their pre-attack levels, while 10 trade below them.

Analysts are now awaiting a detailed response from 2CRSi, expected in the coming days. Despite the current uncertainty, Portzamparc maintains its Buy recommendation with a €60.10 price target.

The situation remains high-risk until the company provides a comprehensive response to the allegations and trading resumes.
